Hey everyone! Update 1.2.0 is released, woohoo!

This adds the much needed organization features for tilesets, map objects, NPCs, audio files, and all other assets! Projects made in PS Maker started getting larger and larger as people were developing in it, so this was a much needed feature.

You can create folders and drag and drop assets into them. These folders will show in the places where the assets are used, such as the Place Map Object and NPC tools.

There's also a new PS Maker icon! Bigggg shout out to Wendigojo for the amazing artwork! It would not be possible without his help. You can find his Instagram and YouTube channel below!

Of course, this update also comes with many other minor improvements and fixes. The full changelog can be found below.
